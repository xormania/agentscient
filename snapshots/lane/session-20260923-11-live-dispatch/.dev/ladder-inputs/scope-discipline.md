# Scope discipline — standing clause for every work order

Owner, 2026-09-10: work orders are going over scope because workers treat
feedback as requirements. This clause is cited by every scope this lane
issues until a mechanical gate replaces it.

1. The requirements are the numbered items in the work order's Contract
   section. Nothing else is a requirement.
2. A reviewer finding is evidence about the contract, not a requirement.
   A finding is actionable in this ladder only when it quotes the numbered
   item it violates. A finding with no cited item is an observation: it is
   recorded in the findings ledger with a disposition and is not fixed here.
3. One fix round per ladder. After it, a new finding is backlog unless it
   stops a numbered item from holding.
4. The frozen test set is the acceptance criterion. Reviews of tests may
   reject an assertion as wrong; they may not add cases. A proposed case is
   a proposed next work order.
5. Reviewers: for every finding, quote the contract item violated or mark
   it observation. The conductor sorts on that field; volume is not sorted
   by the conductor.
6. A correction dispatch names the finding key and the contract item in
   its brief. A correction with neither is refused at the conductor.
