# I-007 — the reserved vocabulary is the product's own here

- recorded: 2026-09-03, while porting the workflow engine and its vocabulary wall
- source: the conductor, from a collision the port surfaced; the owner's rulings I-001 and I-003 decide it
- disposition: open — the wall's mechanism stands with every surface declared a home; this project's lexicon is unruled

## What was found

The House vocabulary wall reserves the dev lane's words — `chain`,
`claim`, `marker`, `packet`, `wf`, `capability` — for the dev lane,
walls `service` outside compose, and `kernel` outside its homes,
because in the House the product is a database whose design documents
must never carry those words. Here the product is the framework: its
law says `capability` in every founding contract, Symfony's `service`
and `Kernel` are the substrate's own nouns, and `wf` is the engine's
name. The House rule, applied here, would wall the product's own
vocabulary out of the product.

## What was done

The mechanism crosses whole and stays registered as a gate; every
surface of this project is declared a home for the reserved words, so
both passes have nothing to wall and stand as mechanism only. The
fixtures and the fault corpus are untouched: they exercise the
mechanism in planted trees, not this tree.

## What it waits on

This project's lexicon — which words are reserved for which surface —
is a ruling nobody has made. When it is made, the homes shrink to what
it says and the wall begins to bite. Until then the term wall (the
House's name) is this repository's only vocabulary gate with teeth.
