# The conductor within a management structure

The conductor is being worked out here as one manager: it takes a scope,
runs the ladder over dispatched workers, lands results, keeps the
ledgers, and stops for the owner where a ruling is needed. The owner
intends it as one level of a structure, not the top of it.

Owner, 2026-09-04, in session: "we're working out how Conductor works -
but i intend use it as a manager that works within a management
structure (next idea is Boss, that dispatches Conductor's - but only in
the final Symfony product)".

Recorded, not designed: a Boss that dispatches conductors, each with a
scope, a budget and a bucket, in the Symfony product only; the Python
lane stays a single conductor. What already holds at every level of
such a structure and need not be reinvented: the workflow genus (a
declared graph with actor classes, evidence duties, failure classes and
gates), the wire (four exit classes, the refusal line), records as the
only memory between levels, the ledgers as the data each level manages
by, and ratification as the one transition reserved for the owner. What
a Boss adds is the allocation: which scope, which conductor, how much,
in which order, judged by the same records the conductor produces
(cost, wall, findings caught and escaped, grants needed). The buckets
(scope item 25) and the findings ledger are the numbers a Boss would
manage by. Not ruled.
