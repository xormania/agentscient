delegated to four support sessions (two Opus, one Sonnet, one wall

and the mechanics of the grant reach the session doing the work without

## What a ruling would have to settle

- the grant carries the issuing session's identity and the owner's words
  in the master session, so the record shows who okayed what and where;
- a grant issued this way names the receiving session (its branch or
  its session id) and is not authority for any other;
- the wall class stays as it is: a wall grant is still declared in the
  session whose deliverable is the wall, and the master session may
  relay the owner's ok but not hold the wall grant itself;
- the hard rows are unchanged (a commit to the trunk, a claim without a
  reading, a ready or merge without proof, a forced push, a prune).
