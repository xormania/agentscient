Where CUE can say it, CUE says it: `new: false` ⇒ `ratified_by: "parent"`
and `cost: "boundary"` ⇒ `boundary_at` present are written as direct
field references inside the definitions, **not** as `if` blocks inside
`close()` (an `if` un-closes the struct — `cue-aar-equality.md` row 4);
each gets a positive and a negative fixture so the construct is proved to
bite, not assumed to.

### 3.1 Economics — what is declared, what is derived, what is refused

| cost | field | who supplies it | refused when |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **write** | `economics.write.minutes` + `tag` | the planner; `measured` from the dispatch wall clock once the Accountant exists, `assumed` until then | absent; non-positive |
| **carry** | *none* — derived | `plan vet --economics`: bytes of the md, the sidecar and `scope.owner_words`, × readers (scope: one harness; inputs: one; tree: all), from the record's `brief.bytes` / `inputs[].sha256` when a record exists | any `carry` key in the sidecar (`plan.derived-key`); `scope.owner_words` over 1024 bytes (`plan.scope-oversize` — the scope is the summary, the record's brief cap) |
| **enforce** | `gates[].cost`, `boundary_at` | the planner, per gate | absent (`plan.gate-cost`); `boundary` with no `boundary_at` |
| **change** | *none* — derived | `plan vet --economics` walks every `*.plan.json` under `.dev/` and lists the sidecars whose `parent.id` is this plan: the revisit cascade, priced as their summed `write.minutes` | any `change` key in the sidecar (`plan.derived-key`) |

The principle: a number the gate can compute is never hand-written,
because a hand-written copy of a computed number is the prose-twice
defect in different clothes.

### 3.2 The standing rule and status

A decision with `new: false` claims to carry nothing new relative to the
parent. The gate makes that claim checkable: every one of its
`premises[].text` must appear verbatim in `parent.premises[].text` or in
some `parent.decisions[].premises[].text`; otherwise `plan.decision-not-new`,
naming the premise. Such a decision is `ratified_by: "parent"` by
construction and never goes to the owner. A decision with `new: true`
is the only kind the owner sees: one sentence (no newline, ≤ 512 bytes —
`plan.decision-multiline`; "one sentence" beyond that is the reviewer's),
premises tagged, `ratified_by` either `pending` or `owner`
(`parent` is refused: `plan.decision-unratified`).

`status` is orthogonal: `standing` is the default; `revisit` is set when a
premise is falsified (by the owner, or by a later plan that measured what
this one assumed) and blocks dependents from `code` exactly as `pending`
does (`plan.stage-blocked`); `superseded` names, in the sentence, what
replaced it. A `superseded` decision is kept, not deleted — the ledger
rule.

## 4. `plan vet` and `plan extract`

`python3 ops/capabilities/checks/plan_vet.py <sub> …`, stdlib only,
`cue` reached by `subprocess`; refusals in the `Refusal(claim, expected=,
actual=, needed=)` shape copied into `ops/capabilities/container/errors.py`
(U3 grows the file; U0 creates it with the `plan.*` names only).

**`vet <path.plan.json> [--definition #Plan] [--economics]`**, fail-fast,
in this order:

1. **raw presence** — `json.load` the document; every `#Plan` key stated
   (`plan.absent-key`); no derived key present (`plan.derived-key`);
   `parent` is a `#Parent` or a `#DeclaredAbsent` and the id is `agentscient`
   iff it is absent (`plan.root-parent`).
2. **`cue vet -c`** against `plan.cue` with the named definition; CUE's
   errors are parsed and re-issued as `plan.<field>` refusals.
3. **relational**, each a named refusal with expected / actual / needed:
   rule 1 digest (`plan.stale-parent`; `plan.bound-prose` when `bound_to:
   prose` and the sidecar exists); rule 2 `deliverables == parent.units[unit].deliverables`
   (`plan.deliverables-mismatch`); rule 3 `settled ⊇` (`plan.settled-dropped`);
   rule 4 no decision id in `settled` (`plan.reopened`); rule 5 scope
   words (`plan.scope-drift`: equal to the parent's, or a superset that
   appends a dated later ruling — the string starts with the parent's);
   rule 6 every parent `open` lands in a unit's `decisions` or `open`
   (`plan.undelegated`); leaf with `open` non-empty (`plan.leaf-open`);
   rule 7 stage (`plan.stage-blocked`); §3.1–3.2 (`plan.gate-cost`,
   `plan.scope-oversize`, `plan.decision-not-new`, `plan.decision-unratified`,
   `plan.decision-multiline`); `plan.gate-half`, `plan.premise-tag`,
   `plan.premise-empty` where CUE cannot say them precisely enough.
4. **drift** — if `<path>.plan.md` exists beside the sidecar, run extract
   in memory and compare bytes (`plan.drift`).

`--definition` is the seam the brief's item 4 asks for and no more: a
later `#Workflow` in the same package is vetted by the same command; U0
does not write `#Workflow` or a fixture for it.

**`extract <path.plan.md> [--check]`** reads exactly: three markdown
tables whose header rows are fixed (`| # | premise | tag | source |`,
`| id | sentence | new | ratified_by | status | premises |`,
`| # | unit | deliverables | stage | needs |`) and one fenced block per
remaining top-level field, tagged ` ```json plan:<field> `. It emits the
sidecar with `sort_keys=True, indent=2` and a trailing newline; `--check`
compares with the committed sidecar and exits 1 on difference. Anything
else in the md is prose and is not read. A field stated twice is refused;
a field stated nowhere is refused — extract never infers.

*This* document is not yet in that form: the form is what U0 defines.
U0's first act is to lay `out/PLAN.md` into `ops/capabilities/plans/U0.plan.md`
in the extract form and `out/U0.plan.json` beside it, and `plan-drift`
(§7) is what proves the two are one document from then on.

**`faults`** runs every `contracts/faults/plan.N.json`: it must differ
from `examples/plan.json` at the site its `.meta.json` names (the plant
is proved), and `vet` on it must fail with stderr matching
`reject_path` — the `vet.py` `check_fault` / `fixture_intact` discipline,
re-implemented not imported.
