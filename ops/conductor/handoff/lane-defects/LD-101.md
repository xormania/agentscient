| defect | A dispatch record that closes carrying findings, and is never landed, puts those findings out of reach of every lever that disposes of them, and nothing reports the gap. Measured on 2026-09-16 in one pass over the live store: 825 records hold a record.json; 576 entries exist under .dev/records/dispatches; 265 live records, 32 percent, have no landed entry. Of those 265, fifty are closed and carry p1 above zero. Of those fifty, eighteen name a head that `git merge-base --is-ancestor <head> origin/dev` accepts, carrying forty p1 findings in all, the oldest closed 2026-09-04T11:45:17Z and the newest 2026-09-16T15:28:03Z. The consequence is not that forty p1 findings are open on the trunk: most are likely to have been fixed in the ordinary course, and this row does not measure that. The consequence is that nothing can say which. `findings.py disposition <record>#<n>` refuses class `key` for every one of them, because `findings.py import` read 945 findings from 210 records and none of these were among them, so the same lever that would record a fix also cannot record that a fix happened. Neither "fixed" nor "open" is available; only "unreachable" is, and no instrument prints it. The instance that raised the row: 20260916T151749Z-review-codex-ef6db7, a security-audit of lane/session-20260916-launcher at head 6213c35a, closed 2026-09-16T15:28:03Z with verdict changes and p1:2 p2:1, whose head merged to dev as PR #254 at 2026-09-16T19:43:11Z, four hours and fifteen minutes after the audit closed and with the audit invisible to every lever that would have held the merge. |
| repair | Not attempted here; the row is the finding. The shape already exists in the lane and wants reusing rather than inventing: `handoff.py held --branch B` exits 1 while a branch holds open threads. The same verb over records — a lever that exits 1 while a closed record carrying findings has no landed entry, and names them — is the mechanism this class needs, because a rule relayed by message is what D-PATTERN-1 forbids. Two places want the reading. The onboard block already prints `dispatches: running=..., newest-closed=...`; a session that is told "running=none" and not told "twelve closed records carrying p1 are unreachable to disposition" has been given a number that reads as an all-clear. The proof gate is the second: a PR whose head is the head of a closed record with an open verdict should refuse ready, since that is exactly the crossing #254 made. Ordering, for whoever takes it: the lever first, then the onboard line, then the gate, because the gate cannot refuse on a reading no lever produces. What must not be done as the repair: landing the 265 records in bulk to make the count zero. That converts an unreachable finding into a finding recorded as untouched, and the disposition of forty p1 findings across twelve days is the work, not the accounting. |
