# LD-88 — a launch that fails after the bind leaves a bound participant with no dispatch

- **class**: coordination provenance
- **opened**: 2026-09-16 by the XO (PT-7) at the Lead's ruling on PR #254
- **status**: open, accepted as a known window; no fix today
- **declared by**: agentscient-db in #254's body, as a behaviour change a
  reviewer should be able to disagree with
- **against**: PR #254 head 6213c35a

## The window

In the launcher's coordination wiring, the bind now happens **before** the
brief, because the MCP config names the participant and the brief names the
verbs. The config cannot be written until the participant exists.

The bind already preceded the child process, so the ordering is not new in
kind. The window is wider: a launch that fails anywhere between the bind and
the child starting leaves a participant in the store carrying dispatch
provenance, for a dispatch that never ran.

## Why it was accepted rather than fixed

The Lead's ruling, 2026-09-16, on #254: the bind-before-brief window gets a
row, not a fix now. The wiring is what unblocks the A-87 relaunch, and the
window is a stale row in the store rather than a wrong answer from it.

## The mechanism, when it is built

The launcher revokes the bind on its own failure path. That is the Lead's
named shape and it belongs to the launcher, not to a caller remembering.

Until then the reading is: a participant with dispatch provenance and no
dispatch record is this window, not a lost worker. Check the dispatch id
before concluding a worker went missing.

## What is not claimed

Nobody has measured how often a launch fails in that span. No such stale row
is known to exist today. This row records an accepted risk declared by its
own author before it cost anything, which is the cheapest moment to write it.
