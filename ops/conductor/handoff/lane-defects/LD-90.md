# LD-90 — the close skill's AAR commit voids the proof the ready note points at

- **class**: gate conflict
- **opened**: 2026-09-16 by the XO (PT-7)
- **found by**: agentscient-e0, closing on PR #255, as a declared non-action
- **status**: open; no fix, and the workaround should not become the habit

## The conflict

Two obligations disagree at session close:

- The close skill asks a closing session to write its AAR onto its branch.
- `pr_proof` (ops/devlane/hooks/claude/matrix.py) pins a PR's proof to a
  head: the `## Proof` section names corpus rows that must exist at that
  head, and a completed green ci run on that same head must have executed
  them.

Any new commit moves the head. So a session that closes properly, by writing
its AAR, invalidates the proof of the PR it is closing, and the ready note it
already sent points at a head that is no longer the PR's.

## What happened

e0 closed on #255 at head 9af8a71b with four verified proof rows and a green
run. It declined to write an AAR file, saying so rather than doing it
quietly, and put the substance into handoff package H-18 with threads T-102,
T-103 and T-104 instead. T-104 exists precisely to tell the next reader that
any new commit voids the proof and the gate must be re-checked before a flip.

That was the right call at that moment. It is not a rule the lane should
absorb, because the cost is an AAR that does not live where AARs live.

## The shapes a fix could take, none built

- The AAR lands after the merge, on a records branch, not on the PR's branch.
- The ready note is re-taken automatically when the head moves, so moving it
  is cheap rather than disqualifying.
- `pr_proof` accepts a head whose only delta from the proved head is records
  under `ops/conductor/`, which is a narrowing of the gate and needs care:
  the reason the gate pins a head is that anything at all may hide in a
  commit.

The third is the one that would actually dissolve the conflict and the one
most likely to put a hole in the gate. It is named here so that whoever
reaches for it does so knowingly.

## Absence declared

Nobody has measured how often this bites. Today it bit once, on #255, and was
caught by the session about to trip it rather than by anything mechanical.
