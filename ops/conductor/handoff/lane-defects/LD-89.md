| defect | An archive bundle limited to unique tips does not restore. Archiving only the commits a branch holds outside the trunk is the obvious saving, and the cheap form is broken. Measured over all 192 unique tips at origin: `git bundle create <f> --all` is 12,077,986 bytes and restores 389 refs and 3,432 commits into an empty repository; `git bundle create <f> <tips> --not origin/dev origin/master` is 3,251,312 bytes and restores 0 refs and 0 commits. The second carries prerequisites. `git bundle verify` names them - d919c982 and ee13221d at the time of measurement - and a fetch into a fresh `git init --bare` errors on each and restores nothing. It matters more than the 8 MB because the failure is silent at write time: the bundle is created without complaint, it has a plausible size, and it sits in the archive looking like insurance. It fails only when someone fetches it into an empty repository, which is the moment after the branches it was meant to preserve are gone from origin. A prerequisite-bearing bundle is not an archive; it is a diff against a trunk that is free to move, be repacked, or itself be rewritten. |
| repair | The rule, stated and standing: archive bundles are self-contained. Every one is verified by restoring it into an empty repository and counting refs and commits before it is written to the archive, and those counts are recorded beside it.
